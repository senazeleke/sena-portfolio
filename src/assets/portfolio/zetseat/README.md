# Zetseat Counseling

**Online counseling & mental wellness platform** — book sessions with professional counselors, join live video/audio calls, explore self-help resources, and read counselor-authored articles in English, Afaan Oromoo, and Amharic.

Built as a full-stack product for **clients**, **counselors**, and **admins**, with multilingual UI (**English · Afaan Oromoo · Amharic**).

Live platform: [zetseatcounseling.com](https://www.zetseatcounseling.com/)

Case study: `/projects/zetseat-counseling`

---

## What it does

Zetseat Counseling helps people get clinical support online without treating therapy as a one-off chat widget. Clients discover counselors and services, book sessions, upload payment proof, and meet over Agora video or audio. Counselors manage requests and availability. Admins run the catalog, moderate content, approve payments, and sell landing-page ad space.

### Roles

| Role | Can do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Client** | Register/login, book sessions, pay with proof, message counselor, join video/audio calls, browse resources & blogs, get live notifications |
| **Counselor** | Profile & availability, accept/manage sessions, join meetings, messenger, submit resources and articles for review |
| **Admin** | Users & services, session overview, approve payments & assign counselors, publish resources, manage landing ads |

---

## Best features

1. **Live video & audio sessions (Agora)** — Real-time counseling rooms with meeting codes and participant controls.
2. **Three-role product with CASL permissions** — Client, counselor, and admin each get a tailored surface.
3. **i18n (EN / OM / AM)** — Full UI localization with cookie-based locale — built for Ethiopia and East Africa.
4. **Resources & articles library** — Curated books, self-help tools, and counselor articles with review workflow.
5. **Screenshot payment proof** — Client uploads receipt → admin approves → counselor assigned — fits local payment habits without card gateway hurdles.
6. **Realtime notifications & messaging** — Socket.IO messenger with unread indicators and trilingual localization.
7. **Landing monetization** — Admin-managed ad carousel (expiry + history) under the hero.
